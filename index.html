This browser can’t scan for Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome on Android or desktop. iPhone browsers don’t support Web Bluetooth. You can still try the demo.
Live signal needs one extra setting. In Chrome, open chrome://flags, turn on “Experimental Web Platform features,” then reload.
Good to know
  • Distance comes from signal strength. The blip’s direction around the radar is not measured.
  • Devices only appear if they’re on and advertising. Earbuds in their case won’t show up.
  • Walls and your body change the signal, so treat distance as a rough guide.
  • Nothing leaves your browser.
